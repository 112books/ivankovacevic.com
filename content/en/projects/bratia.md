Bratia, the Barcelona-based band, was born from the need of the Serbian double bassist settled in Barcelona, <a href="https://ivankovacevic.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Ivan Kovacevic</a>, to bring two musical worlds together in a thrilling proposition. The infinite sonic world of the Balkan countries and the energy, sobriety and precision of jazz manouche, unified by the nomadism of gypsy music, create an explosive cocktail that leaves no one indifferent…

As one of the pillars of the emblematic band <a href="https://bgko.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Barcelona Gipsy Balkan Orchestra</a>, Kovacevic, with the experience accumulated over years in more than 35 countries, together with his two battle companions, the Greek percussionist Stelios Togias and the violinist from Tarragona Pere Nolasc Turu, embarks on this adventure forming a quintet with two guitarists seasoned in the legacy of Django Reinhardt. The Mallorcan Biel Ballester, an artist who has been sweeping stages all over the world for years with the trio under his name, and Victor Paradis, of Canadian origin, a young promise who in a short time has become an essential of the music scene in the Catalan capital.

The blend of traditional music from the Balkans, Eastern Europe and the Near East with the language of the ensemble's own songs makes this quintet a unique proposition, a sonic universe, a brotherhood. As the name itself, of Slavic origin, indicates… BRATIA
