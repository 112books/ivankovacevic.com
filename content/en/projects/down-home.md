### Genre

neo swing, crazy bluesin’, shaky rhythm, groovy boogie

### Lineup

Hernán Senra (Chino): dobro and vocals\
Duska Miscevic, alto sax\
Pol Prats: tenor sax\
Ivan Kovacevic, double bass\
Marti Elias, drums

### Record label

El toro records – Formula free – Indie

Down Home is one of the best groups Barcelona has brought forth in recent years. They started out inspired by the swing and jump blues of the 1950s, but over more than 6 years they have developed an unmistakable sound with an identity of its own.
