## What are cookies?

A cookie is a small text file stored in your device's browser when you visit a website. It contains information about your browsing, preferences or content customisation. Similar technologies exist that also collect data about browsing activity and are included under the term "cookies" for the purposes of this document.

## What cookies does this website use?

This is a static informational website designed to work **without analytics or advertising cookies**.

- **First-party cookies:** no first-party analytics, advertising or personalisation cookies requiring consent are installed. Only strictly technical cookies or storage needed for the site to work may be used (for example, remembering your language preference), which are exempt from the consent requirement.
- **Cookie-less analytics:** visit statistics are obtained with GoatCounter, which does not use cookies and does not collect identifying personal data.
- **Third-party cookies:** third-party cookies may be set through embedded content (for example YouTube or Spotify players). These are managed by those providers.

Because no first-party analytics or advertising cookies are used, this site **does not show a cookie consent banner**.

## Third-party embedded content

Some pages may include players or other embedded content from YouTube, Spotify or other platforms. When loaded, these services may set their own cookies and process data under their own policies. You can find more information at:

- [Google (YouTube) privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [types of cookies used by Google](https://policies.google.com/technologies/types).
- [Spotify privacy policy](https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

For more on data processing, see the [privacy policy](/en/legal/privacy-policy/).

## How to disable or delete cookies

You can restrict, block or delete cookies from this and other websites by changing your browser settings. These settings differ for each browser. Instructions for the most common browsers:

- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enhanced-tracking-protection-firefox-desktop)
- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)

Blocking third-party cookies may affect how embedded players work.
